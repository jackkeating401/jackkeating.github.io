About me

Hi, I'm Jack. I am a first year PhD student at Harvard University. I am on the Politics and Institutions track at the John F. Kennedy School of Government, where I study international relations and comparative politics. My research interests lie broadly in international security and political violence. I focus primarily on questions related to the intersection of domestic political violence and foreign policy decision making.

Before coming to Harvard, I held internships in the Department of Defense and the Center for a New American Security. I was also a James C. Gaither Junior Fellow in the American Statecraft Program at the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace during the 2023-2024 academic year. I hold a BA in Government from Dartmouth College, and am originally from Sun Valley, Idaho. In my free time, I love spending time in the mountains and playing tennis, basketball, soccer, and golf. Please don't hesitate to reach out if you want to discuss research or any of these things!
